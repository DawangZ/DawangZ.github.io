Synthesis of Large-Scale Concentric Ring Arrays for High Sparsity and Wide Scanning via Alternating Optimization

Heng Zhao, Yong-Ling Ban, Lujie Zhou, Yanhui Liu, Jun Hu

IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation, 73(11): 8863–8875, 2025.

DOI: 10.1109/TAP.2025.3591985 ↗来源 ↗
本页内容英文原文摘要BibTeX 引用研究方向

英文原文摘要

While phased arrays (PAs) offer flexible beam forming and wide-angle scanning capabilities, the high cost of transmit/receive (T/R) modules significantly limits the development of large-scale arrays. This study addresses the optimization design problem of large-scale sparse concentric ring arrays (SCRAs) for wide-angle scanning with suppressed sidelobes under practical constraints. To address the challenge of co-optimizing array sparsity and beam scanning performance, this study first introduces the concept of the sparsity lower bound (SLB) to thoroughly account for the effect of neglecting higher order harmonics on approximation calculations. Next, by decoupling the dual optimization variables (ring radius and weights) in the sidelobe minimization problem, an innovative array configuration design framework based on alternating iterative optimization is proposed. To increase algorithm robustness, a Gompertz curve (GC) model is introduced to establish an efficient initial solution selection mechanism. Multiple examples show that the array optimization achieved by the proposed method is highly competitive. The most notable achievement is the achievement of a wide-scan sparse CRA with 176697 elements (35.08% of the fully populated array), an ultralow sidelobe level of −48.48 dB, and an optimization time of only 732 s.

中文导读

面向实际约束下的大规模稀疏同心圆环阵，研究如何兼顾高稀疏度、宽角扫描和低旁瓣。论文引入稀疏下界，将环半径与权重交替优化，并以Gompertz曲线辅助初始解选择；摘要报告的是数值设计实例，不能表述为硬件实测性能。
