A little background.

A background in electronic science, with a focus on the geometry and optimization of antenna arrays.

PhD Student · Electronic Science and Technology
University of Electronic Science and Technology of China
Chengdu, China

I am a PhD student in Electronic Science and Technology at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). My research connects mathematical optimization with array geometry and radiation-pattern requirements. I am interested in using limited aperture and hardware resources effectively, while keeping the physical constraints of an array explicit.
