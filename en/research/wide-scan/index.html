Research area 03

Wide-angle scanning

How can a sparse ring array preserve its scanning behavior?

On this pageApproachAuthorship & contributionPublication evidenceScope & limitations

Approach

Concentric ring geometry supplies useful structure for array synthesis. My work studies ring attitude correction and alternating optimization as ways to address scanning requirements.

Authorship & contribution

My first-authored publications examine scanning-beam synthesis for sparse concentric ring arrays. Related papers separate uniform-amplitude design from high-sparsity, wide-scanning synthesis.
