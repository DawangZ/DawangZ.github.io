Research area 02

Irregular array design

How can subarray structure serve an irregular aperture?

On this pageApproachAuthorship & contributionPublication evidenceScope & limitations

Approach

An evolutionary strategy searches tiling arrangements for large irregular arrays. The design problem links the shape and arrangement of subarrays to the resulting array synthesis task.

Authorship & contribution

My first-authored work proposes a tiling technique based on an evolutionary strategy for large-scale irregular arrays. The paper is the source for the algorithm and its evaluated configurations.

Publication evidence

Scope & limitations

Tiling choices are evaluated for the configurations and objectives in the paper. Manufacturing, feed-network, and full-wave effects require evaluation for a particular implementation.
