Research area 01

Sparse array synthesis

How can an array use its aperture more efficiently?

On this pageApproachAuthorship & contributionPublication evidenceScope & limitations

Approach

Array geometry and optimization are treated together. My published work investigates mutation-based search for large sparse planar arrays and alternating optimization for concentric ring arrays.

Authorship & contribution

My first-authored papers develop synthesis algorithms for sparse planar and concentric ring arrays. The linked publications provide the formulations, comparison conditions, and numerical evidence.

Scope & limitations

The conclusions depend on the array model, aperture, scan range, and constraints studied in each paper. A synthesis result is not a claim of universal hardware performance.
